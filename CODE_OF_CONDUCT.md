# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and users of Post-Training RSI Pipeline are expected to communicate with respect, technical honesty, and care for other people's time and safety.

Constructive behavior includes:

- discussing ideas, evidence, and tradeoffs rather than attacking people;
- stating uncertainty, limitations, conflicts of interest, and failed experiments;
- giving actionable review feedback;
- respecting privacy, intellectual property, licenses, and data-handling restrictions;
- making the project accessible to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience;
- correcting mistakes without retaliation.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, or unwanted personal attention;
- insults, intimidation, public shaming, or sustained disruption;
- doxxing or publishing private communications without permission;
- knowingly submitting malware, secrets, fabricated evidence, plagiarized material, or misleading claims;
- using project channels to target third parties or coordinate abuse.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository Issues, Pull Requests, reviews, Discussions, release channels, project events, and private communications made in a project role.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer route in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Do not publish sensitive personal details in a public Issue. Security incidents belong in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, request a correction, issue a warning, restrict participation, or permanently ban an account. Decisions consider severity, impact, pattern, intent, remediation, and community safety. A maintainer involved in a complaint should recuse when another qualified reviewer is available.

No policy can guarantee a particular outcome. Reports will be handled in good faith and with the minimum disclosure needed to resolve the matter.
