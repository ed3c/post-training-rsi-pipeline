# Maintainers

## Current maintainers

| GitHub | Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| [`@ed3c`](https://github.com/ed3c) | Owner and lead maintainer | Direction, access control, issue triage, review, security coordination, releases, and final merge decisions |

## Adding a maintainer

A new maintainer should demonstrate sustained contribution, sound judgment at the project's trust boundaries, reliable review, respect for privacy and licenses, and the ability to maintain English and Traditional Chinese public documentation.

The owner records delegated authority in a dedicated Pull Request. Repository write access alone does not imply release, security, or policy authority.

## Removing or becoming inactive

Access may be removed for inactivity, security risk, conduct violations, loss of role, or at the maintainer's request. Open responsibilities should be transferred before removal where practical.

## Bus-factor controls

Critical policy, release, signing, recovery, and data-handling procedures should be documented in the repository rather than kept only in personal memory. Secrets and recovery material must not be committed.
